# Water War Tiers — Draft

**Status:** Shipped in v3.3.0; da descriptions live in index.html (WATER_WAR_TIERS) now.
**Date:** 2026-09-24

Da warm-season partner to da 288 snow war tiers. **222 tiers**, driven by heat:
a city lands on its tier by da same heat score as da heat impact ladder, so hotter,
longer, and more unusual heat means a bigger water war. Past 134°F da ladder gets keyed
on air temperature and goes fictional, matchin' da heat impact ladder.

★ marks da runnin' storyline, **da Pump**: one kid's squirt-gun sketch dat grows into a
pumpin' empire, right alongside da snow war's SnowSpreader design record.

## Voice

Plain, straight-ahead sentences, da snow war register, wit' **lots of concrete numbers**
and **a little funnier** (set by Jill on The Next Rain). Deadpan, never winkin'. Near
da deadly top of da realistic range (Shade Treaty → Dhahran Accord), da tiers turn
from play to survival instead of jokin' about lethal heat.

## Descriptions written so far

**The Next Rain** (tier 222, da end cap)

> Da universe cools down below about 3,600°F, and water can hold together for da first time
> since da last war ended. Da first molecules form around 200 million years in, in da
> leftovers of stars dat never find out what dey were for. 9.1 billion years later, on
> some rock nobody named, 7,900 miles across, it rains: about two miles of it, over roughly a
> thousand years, which is da longest refill queue ever on da books. Nuttin's alive to
> throw any of it. Da ledger resets to No Water Drawn, and 4.4 billion years after dat,
> on graph paper at four squares to da inch, somebody draws a pump.
