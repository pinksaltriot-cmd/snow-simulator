# Water War Tiers — Draft

**Status:** Draft names, not yet in the app. Heat Index Mode Wave 2a.
**Date:** 2026-09-24

The warm-season counterpart to the 286 snow war tiers. **222 tiers**, driven by heat:
a city lands on its tier by the same heat score as the heat impact ladder, so hotter,
longer, and more unusual heat means a bigger water war. Past 134°F the ladder is keyed
on air temperature and turns fictional, matching the heat impact ladder.

★ marks the running storyline, **the Pump**: one kid's squirt-gun sketch that grows into a
pumping empire, parallel to the snow war's SnowSpreader design record.

## Voice

Plain declarative sentences, the snow war register, with **lots of concrete numbers**
and **slightly funnier** (set by Jill on The Next Rain). Deadpan, never winking. Near
the deadly top of the realistic range (Shade Treaty → Dhahran Accord), the tiers turn
from play to survival rather than joking about lethal heat.

## Descriptions written so far

**The Next Rain** (tier 222, the end cap)

> The universe cools below about 3,600°F, and water can hold together for the first time
> since the last war ended. The first molecules form around 200 million years in, in the
> leftovers of stars that never find out what they were for. 9.1 billion years later, on
> an unnamed rock 7,900 miles across, it rains: about two miles of it, over roughly a
> thousand years, which is the longest refill queue ever recorded. Nothing is alive to
> throw any of it. The ledger resets to No Water Drawn, and 4.4 billion years after that,
> on graph paper at four squares to the inch, someone draws a pump.
